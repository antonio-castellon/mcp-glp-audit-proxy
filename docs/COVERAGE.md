# Regulatory coverage: mcp-glp-audit-proxy

Status: draft, 2026-10-18. Source of truth for clauses: STANDARDS.md (see its Verification log).
Intended location: docs/COVERAGE.md; section 4 is the README "Regulatory coverage" summary.

## 1. What this document says (plain language)

mcp-glp-audit-proxy sits between an AI host and your MCP servers and keeps a tamper-evident record of
every tool call. It is **designed to support** the audit-trail and data-integrity expectations of the
frameworks listed below (OECD GLP, Swiss GLPV, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, MHRA, PIC/S, WHO,
EU AI Act and related technical standards).

The product is designed to support those expectations. Meeting them depends on how you validate,
configure and operate it inside your own quality system, with your own SOPs, training and reviews.
This document shows, clause by clause, which feature helps with which requirement, and what stays
your responsibility (section 3).

How to read the matrix:
- **Edition**: Community = open-source proxy core, local JSONL hash chain, verify CLI, tool
  allowlist/denylist. Pro = PostgreSQL/MSSQL and S3 Object Lock backends, Ed25519/ECDSA chain signing,
  RFC 3161 timestamps, PDF/CSV auditor reports, IQ/OQ validation
  pack, SSO/OAuth, old/new value capture, a Merkle log, OpenTelemetry spans, DE/FR/EN report
  templates, and the GLP Lab Data Pack for mcp-advanced-excel (provenance hashes, instrument
  templates, advanced LOD/LOQ and molar units, batch processing, Ed25519 signed output). Pro is
  available as a licensed / paid service - contact [antonio@castellon.ch](mailto:antonio@castellon.ch).
  "Backlog" means there is no commitment in either edition. "Out of scope" means neither edition
  provides it. Personal Part 11 e-signatures are out of scope. Community is tamper-evident via the
  hash chain. It is not signed. Signing is Pro.
- **Status**: Verified = clause checked against the primary source (or, for Swiss law and eIDAS, against
  the official fedlex/EUR-Lex text) on 2026-10-18. Unverified = paywalled or secondary-source only; do
  not quote to customers until checked.
- **Priority**: P0 = must for the first release, P1 = should, P2 = may (from STANDARDS.md section 4).
- Drafts (EU GMP Annex 11 2025, EU GMP Annex 22, EU GMP Chapter 4) are marked "not in force"; they show where inspections
  are heading, not current law.

## 3. Customer responsibilities / not covered

The proxy provides technical controls. The following remain with the customer (the regulated company):

- **Computerised system validation**: deciding the GAMP category, writing the URS, executing IQ/OQ/PQ in
  your environment, and approving the validation report. The Pro validation pack is input, not a
  substitute (OECD No. 17, Part 11 11.10(a), PIC/S 9.2-9.3).
- **SOPs and quality system**: procedures for audit-trail review (who, what, how often), incident and
  deviation handling, change control, periodic review, backup/restore and disaster recovery.
- **Audit-trail review itself**: the proxy gives filters, exports and flags; qualified, independent
  reviewers (QA involvement for GLP) must actually perform and document the review.
- **Training and qualification** of users, approvers, admins and QA (Part 11 11.10(i)).
- **Accountability policies** if a process outside this product uses electronic signatures, including
  the one-time certification to FDA that those signatures are the legal equivalent of handwritten
  signatures (Part 11 11.10(j), 11.100(c)). This product does not provide personal Part 11 e-signatures.
- **Identity and access management**: running the IdP, issuing unique accounts, removing leavers,
  periodic access reviews, assigning roles and keeping admins out of GxP work.
- **Key custody**: generating, storing (KMS/HSM), rotating and backing up signing keys; keeping old
  public keys for the full retention period; choosing and contracting a (qualified) TSA.
- **Retention policy configuration**: setting retention per study or record type to meet national law
  (e.g. at least 10 years after study completion under Swiss GLPV; at least as long as the subject
  records under Part 11; at least 6 months under the AI Act), applying legal holds, and approving disposal.
- **Infrastructure**: operating the database, S3 account (incl. protection against account deletion),
  time synchronisation (NTP), TLS certificates, network security and monitoring.
- **Data protection**: lawful basis, DPIA, and the choice of redaction/pseudonymisation settings, which
  must be balanced against GxP "original record" expectations (GDPR / Swiss nFADP).
- **Regulatory classification**: whether a given AI use is "high-risk" under the EU AI Act, and whether
  it is a critical GMP application (Annex 22 draft excludes LLMs from critical use).
- **Data in the upstream systems**: the proxy records what passes through it; audit trails inside your
  ELN, LIMS, instruments and databases remain required and are not replaced.
- **Archiving organisation**: archivist role, archive access control and records of archive movements
  beyond the proxy's own logs.
